About the trainer

Is Advantage Play BJ free?

Yes. It runs locally in a modern browser with no account or subscription.

Which counting system does it teach?

The trainer uses the balanced Hi-Lo system: 2–6 are +1, 7–9 are 0, and tens through aces are −1.

Does the EV simulator guarantee casino results?

No. It provides reproducible Monte Carlo estimates for the selected rules and spread. Variance and real-world conditions still matter.
