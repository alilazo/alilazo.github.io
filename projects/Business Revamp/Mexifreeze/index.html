From Airstream to Ice Cream

Where Sweet Memories Begin

Pause and savor the nectar of life — in the form of ice cream.

From rich ice cream and sweet, tangy mangonadas to refreshing water-based paletas and flavorful snacks, every bite is crafted with care and served from our iconic Airstream.

At MexiFreeze, our story begins with a passion for creating handcrafted frozen treats that bring people together. Serving from our charming retro Airstream, we offer ice cream and refreshing, water-based paletas made with real fruit and quality ingredients — because every scoop should feel like something worth remembering.

We believe life’s sweetest memories are made over a scoop of ice cream — whether it’s with family, friends, or just treating yourself. Our mission is to craft joyful, delicious experiences one scoop at a time — and we invite you to join us on this delicious journey!
